# DOLESS AI × The Unscoreable Football Challenge 🎯

- Member 2: Alfred Shaju - Sahrdaya College of Engineering and Technology

### Project Description
A two-project combo of elaborate uselessness. DOLESS AI is a fake AI homework-avoidance system that runs you through a full analysis dashboard — terminal output, stats, escape plans, a chatbot, a leaderboard — just to tell you not to do your homework. Once the analysis is "complete", it redirects you to The Unscoreable Football Challenge: a fully playable 3D six-a-side football match where the goalposts physically dodge out of the way every time the ball gets close. The final score is always 0 – 0. Nobody has ever scored. Nobody ever will.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Students everywhere are accidentally becoming productive. They open their laptops, see their homework, and — against all instinct — start doing it. This is a crisis. Simultaneously, football fans around the world keep watching matches where someone eventually scores, robbing them of the pure existential peace of a match that simply cannot end in anything.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
We built an unnecessarily sophisticated AI system that accepts your homework task, runs it through a fake terminal with eleven deeply concerning diagnostic steps, assigns you a Procrastination Score™, generates a seven-step escape plan, and officially recommends you do not start. Then — as a final directive — it sends you to a 3D football stadium where the goalposts are sentient cowards. They see the ball coming and run. Every single time. The scoreboard tracks how many times the posts have fled, because that's the only number that will ever change.

## Technical Details
### Technologies/Components Used
For Software:
- HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+)
- No frameworks — 100% vanilla
- Three.js r128 (via CDN) for the 3D football game
- Google Fonts (Space Mono, Syne), npx serve for local dev

For Hardware:
- A laptop or desktop with a modern browser
- A keyboard (WASD / arrow keys for football controls)
- Optional: a mouse, for clicking the "PLAY INSTEAD" button with dramatic flair
